The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog, however, was not amused!
It barked: "Fox? FOX!" and the fox ran away. Quick thinking saved the fox.
Dogs and foxes are not friends; the lazy dog knows it. 여우와 개는 친구가 아니다. 여우는 빠르다, 개는 느리다.
